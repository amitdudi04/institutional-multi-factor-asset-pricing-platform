# Final Platform Architecture

The v1.0.0 platform is a six-layer authenticated research chain:

1. immutable, reproducible institutional data ingestion and promotion;
2. point-in-time factor construction and diagnostics;
3. classical asset-pricing estimation and inference;
4. constrained portfolio construction, accounting, risk, costs, and scenarios;
5. temporally safe explainable machine-learning research;
6. authenticated API, dashboard, reporting, observability, deployment, and CI delivery.

Each downstream phase authenticates its parent evidence and binds configuration, Git identity, schema, checksums, validation, and lineage. Delivery clients do not access arbitrary Parquet, DuckDB, model, or local filesystem paths. The platform is research software, not a trading system, advisory service, enterprise identity platform, or cloud production service.

Reproducibility depends on approved source rights, explicit open empirical decisions, locked software, immutable publications, and recorded limitations. A passing software suite does not establish empirical alpha or live suitability.
