# Admissions Project Summary

I built and empirically exercised an institutional-style quantitative research platform with immutable data lineage, point-in-time SEC fundamentals, effective-dated security identity, 48 factor definitions, robust asset-pricing inference, portfolio/ML governance gates, and authenticated API/dashboard/report delivery.

For the final free/public-data run, the platform screened 822 market candidates and accepted 487 defensibly bounded equities. The real panel contains 715,447 daily observations and supports 47 estimable factors plus five configured asset-pricing model families. Real data exposed three cross-phase defects; I corrected them with regression tests without weakening methodology.

The project’s most important result is disciplined uncertainty. It refuses to fabricate terminal returns, historical mappings, shares, costs, or ML targets. Consequently, Phase 4 net economics and Phase 5 ML remain explicitly non-estimable until their owner decisions are approved. The work is research software, not financial advice and not a claim of proprietary-data equivalence.
