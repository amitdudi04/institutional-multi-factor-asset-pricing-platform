# Executive Research Memo

All 822 HF stock candidates were classified. The final frozen universe contains 487 equities and 715,447 daily observations, with median daily breadth 428. Expanded Phase 1, all configured Phase 2 factors, the five configured Phase 3 model families, and real Phase 6 delivery passed authentication.

The central empirical result is capability, not investment performance: the free/public evidence can support a large bounded cross-section when identity, splice, share basis, and filing-time availability are enforced. Forty-seven factors are estimable; equity issuance is not. Multifactor models fit the project diagnostic portfolios better in-sample than CAPM, but this is not sufficient to claim stable alpha or economic value.

Phase 4 and Phase 5 remain non-estimable. Transaction costs are unset, and ML target/horizon/benchmark/features are unset. Filling those values with zero or inferred choices would violate governance. Docker and GitHub/merge/tag work were explicitly deferred. Recommended owner action is to approve and preregister the remaining Phase 4–5 decisions before any net-performance or ML claim.
