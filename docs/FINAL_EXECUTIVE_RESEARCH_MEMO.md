# Executive Research Memo

The final local study classified all 822 HF stock candidates and froze 487 defensibly bounded equities before analytical performance was examined. The authenticated chain now spans expanded Phase 1, 47 estimable Phase 2 factors, five Phase 3 model families, eight Phase 4 portfolio methods, eight Phase 5 walk-forward models, and Phase 6 API/dashboard/report delivery.

The strongest positive result is engineering evidence, not alpha. Multifactor models fit project diagnostic portfolios better than CAPM in-sample. Frozen costs materially reduce every portfolio result, and constrained allocators exhibit different risk and tracking trade-offs. However, the portfolio test spans only 32 months and uses factor portfolios rather than directly investable security portfolios.

Machine learning does not establish stable incremental value. All aggregate IC bootstrap intervals include zero. Only Random Forest has a slightly positive mean after-cost fold return, and that result is positive in just 9 of 17 folds. Explanation rankings vary across models and methods and are non-causal.

The formal decisions are H1 and H3 inconclusive, H2 and H5 partially supported, H4 supported, and H6-H7 not supported. `equity_issuance` remains non-estimable rather than zero-filled. The completed work is a reproducible academic research study, not financial advice, a production trading system, or CRSP/Compustat equivalence. The validated empirical state is preserved by `research-empirical-public-v1` and `project-complete-public-data-v1`; later portfolio-documentation commits do not move those tags.
