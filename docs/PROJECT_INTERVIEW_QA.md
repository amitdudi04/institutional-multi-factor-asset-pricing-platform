# Project Interview Q&A

## Why were only 487 of 822 candidates accepted?

Candidates failed exact attribution/splice, historical identity, singular share allocation, split-basis compatibility, terminal-event, instrument/venue, or governed fundamental-projection rules. The universe was frozen before downstream performance was examined.

## How were survivorship and look-ahead controlled?

Active and delisted lifecycle evidence was retained; unresolved terminal treatment caused rejection. Current SEC mappings were not backfilled. Fundamentals preserve filing availability, accession, taxonomy, concept, and unit. Phase 5 uses monthly decisions, purging, a one-month embargo, and a 21-session future target.

## What did real data uncover?

It exposed benchmark-calendar, sparse-regression, explainability-routing, economic-allocation, manifest-lineage, and monthly-sampling defects. Focused regressions now protect each corrected boundary.

## Did the factors or models generate alpha?

No stable alpha claim is justified. Multifactor models improve in-sample adjusted R-squared relative to CAPM, but Phase 5 IC intervals include zero and after-cost economic gains are unstable. The sole slightly positive mean ML result, Random Forest, is positive in only 9 of 17 folds.

## Why freeze costs before performance?

Pre-performance freezing prevents choosing costs to preserve attractive results. LOW/BASE/HIGH one-way totals are 5/10/20 bps. The implementation avoids a fabricated nonlinear market-impact estimate because authenticated liquidity is unavailable.

## What is the main portfolio finding?

All eight methods produce positive net returns over the available 32-month window, but method rankings and risk trade-offs differ and costs reduce every result. The window and factor-portfolio test assets are too limited for a persistence or trading claim.

## Are SHAP results causal?

No. They describe fitted-model behavior. Feature rank changes between Random Forest permutation and SHAP, so the study rejects stable cross-method explanation.
