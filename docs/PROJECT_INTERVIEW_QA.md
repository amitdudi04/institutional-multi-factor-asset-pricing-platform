# Project Interview Q&A

## Why were only 487 of 822 candidates accepted?

Candidates failed exact HF attribution/splice, historical identity, singular share allocation, split-basis compatibility, terminal-event, instrument/venue, or governed projection rules. Rejections were evidence-driven and occurred before performance analysis.

## How was survivorship handled?

Alpha active/delisted snapshots were retained, but accepted delisted candidates required resolved terminal treatment. Current SEC mappings were not backfilled; usable periods begin at exact tagged filing anchors. The resulting panel is still source-availability selected and is disclosed as such.

## How was look-ahead prevented?

Fundamentals preserve period end, filing/availability date, accession, taxonomy, concept, and unit. Market rows receive only shares and fundamentals available by their timestamp. Fiscal periods ending before the verified identity interval are excluded.

## What did real data uncover?

It uncovered complete-case and rolling-window bugs in Phase 3 and a benchmark-calendar bug in Phase 2. Regression tests now cover all three.

## Did the factors or models generate alpha?

The platform generated descriptive factor and regression estimates. They do not establish causal or deployable alpha. Net costs are not approved, and Phase 4/5 economic validation is not estimable.

## Why not assume zero costs or infer an ML target?

Those are owner decisions in the governing specification. Inferring them after observing data would violate preregistration and model-risk controls.
